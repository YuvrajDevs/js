# Global English-language AI builder / vibe coder / AI automation roles (2026)

**Method and honesty note.** Today is 2026-11-03. WebFetch was blocked for every site tried (remotive, jobgether, jobicy, arc.dev, builtin: DNS not allowed in the sandbox). All evidence is SEARCH SNIPPET ONLY; no posting was read in full. I ran about 14 searches and identified about 35 distinct postings or posting families, well short of the 60+ target. The counts below are therefore indicative, not statistically meaningful. Snippets are model-summarized, so exact quotes are limited to what the summaries reproduced. Many postings are undated in the snippets, and mid-2026 postings may be closed by Nov 2026.

## 1. What do vibe coder / AI prototype / AI automation postings ask for?

### Takeaway
Postings consistently ask for hands-on use of agentic coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor) and no-code app builders (Lovable, Bolt, Replit), plus a shipped-project portfolio, fast MVP delivery and ownership. Automation roles add n8n/Make/Zapier, APIs, webhooks, JSON and LLM/RAG work.
