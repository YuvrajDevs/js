# Remote/overseas-friendly AI builder roles in Japan and South Korea (2026)

Method and limits: 5 WebSearch calls and 3 WebFetch attempts. Every WebFetch failed (japan-dev.com and www.tokyodev.com do not resolve in this sandbox; they would need to be allowed in the session's network settings). All findings below are "snippet only": they come from search-result summaries of aggregator pages, not from reading the postings. Posted dates, experience and eligibility are mostly unverified. Sample size is about 10 postings, so treat it as anecdotal, not a market measure. No pay figures were captured except one.

### Takeaway
Senior or multi-year requirements dominate: Money Forward is Staff, the fully remote CareerCross role needs 3+ years, and Sendbird and Cohere are senior or lead. Junior signals are limited to カサナレ L1-L2 and the unverified Remote Rocketship "1-2 years" claim.

### Gaps
