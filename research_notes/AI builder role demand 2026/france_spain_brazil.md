## Which French/Spanish/Brazilian employers hire worldwide-remote juniors/contractors with English as the working language?

### Inferences
- A candidate in India is likely ineligible for most of these. LATAM and Brazil roles are residence-locked, usually for time-zone and contractor-payment reasons. EU roles are locked by EU or CET time zone.
- Junior-friendly roles are rare in the sample. Only BrandBastion (Spain), Lighthouse (Spain) and the Cadiz posting are explicitly junior, and all are country-locked.
- Brazil "worldwide" tags (TechMagic, Jobgether) are the only possible fits and need checking on the primary pages.

### Gaps
- No primary-page reads. Jobgether, Remote Rocket Ship, Welcome to the Jungle, Wellfound, startup.jobs and HN Who's Hiring were not accessed.
- No posting dates for most items, and no exact experience or language quotes beyond those above.
- No French employers confirmed as worldwide-remote.
- The "vibe coder / AI-native builder" title was not found at any French, Spanish or Brazilian employer. The only near-match is the JobTarget "AI-native Software Developer", which is a regional listing.

## Region-locked vs worldwide

### Takeaway
Region-locked: Brazil-only (Loadsmart, Adaptive Teams, Aloware, RM Educacao), Brazil/Argentina/Portugal (Pyyne), LATAM-only (Get on Board reposts), EU/EMEA (Pragmatike, Extenteam, Jobgether EU), Spain-only (Lighthouse, BrandBastion Barcelona), France/Spain (360Learning).
